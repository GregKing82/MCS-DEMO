Load Board and Routing

Built for: Thumb Regional Supply Co. · drag the day’s orders onto trucks, see every stop against the clock and the customer’s window, then lock the run in

Try it: grab an order on the left and drop it on a truck. Drag a stop up or down to change the order of the run, or back to the left to unassign it. Each truck has its own departure time. Too much for one trip? Add a second run and it leaves after the truck is back and reloaded. When a run looks right, Finalize and lock it. That texts the customers and freezes the run.

Inventory and Reorder

Built for: Marlette Ag & Supply · sales movement drives a usage forecast, and the forecast drives when and what to order

Try it: record sales and watch the weekly sales line, the forecast, the days of cover and the runs-out date move. Switch the forecast method or how many weeks of supply to carry and the suggested purchase orders change. Items whose forecast demand over the lead time will outrun what is on hand and on order get flagged for a reorder. Orders placed in the Orders tool feed the same sales history.
